Chapter 4 From Hell It Rose is the first major trial chapter. There are **23 collectibles**, with several light/spike puzzles.

## Collectible Overview

| Type | Count | Details |
|---|---|---|
| Blade | 1 | Kopis |
| Artefact | 3 | Polished Cornicello / Horned Rhyton / Grand Mosaic |
| Charm | 2 | Arm Band #2 / Trinket #1 |
| Resonance Points | 17 | — |

## Route (5 sub-chapters)

1. **A familiar threat** — three-symbol puzzle door
2. **Down the abyss** — mirror puzzle + maze puzzle
3. **Puzzles and traps** — spike traps + Kopis
4. **At the bottom** — large lever room (Returning the Favor achievement)
5. **Theseus** — bull mirror puzzle + combat

## Puzzle Solutions

- **Three-symbol puzzle**: shine the orb at the pedestal, follow the blue line on the floor to see the matching symbol on the wall, then rotate all 3 pedestals to show it. Solution (left to right): Y - C - ↑.
- **Mirror puzzle**: left mirror → right mirror → front-left mirror → front-right mirror → door symbol.
- **Maze puzzle**: left = "O" / right = "I".
- **Spike traps**: platform 1 = left-left-left; platform 2 = right-middle-middle; spike wall = the third slot from the left.
- **Bull mirror puzzle**: pair the white floor symbols against the red base, two at a time. First pair (bell + T): right mirror shines on the 1st icon on the front-left wall, left mirror on the 3rd icon on the back-right wall. Second pair (reverse-S + triangle): left mirror on the 3rd on the front-right wall, right mirror on the 4th on the front-left wall. After the bull lowers its hand, shine its armband for the new symbols "h" and "w", then match them too.

## Artefact Locations

- **Polished Cornicello** (Present): after the first cliff, climb down the ladder.
- **Horned Rhyton** (Past): in the puzzle room after the spike traps, immediately turn left around the corner.
- **Grand Mosaic** (Past): right after the long zipline, turn left and jump the gap.

## Blade Location

- **Kopis**: after the spike traps, interact with the pedestal to open the tomb, then descend and open the large chest (almost impossible to miss). Technique "Stagger Blow": the final hit of a combo causes a big stagger.

## Chapter Achievements

- From Hell It Rose (complete Chapter 4)
- Returning the Favor (in the large lever room, throw a dropped knife at the archer)
