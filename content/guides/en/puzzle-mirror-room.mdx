The mirror-room puzzle behind the "O over a line" door in Chapter 5 — align three colored lights to the central pillar's dots.

## Quick Solution

Reflect the three colors onto the central pillar's matching dots:

1. **Red**: shine the rightmost mirror → shine the right side of the pillar
2. **Blue**: left mirror → back-lower mirror → middle mirror → left side of the pillar
3. **Green**: back-right mirror → back of the pillar

After the pillar lowers, use the pedestal that appears.

## How It Works

- The goal is to reflect red/blue/green onto the central pillar's matching colored dots
- Blue has the longest path (through 4 mirrors) — reflect step by step
- All three aligned lowers the pillar and reveals the pedestal

> Why you're stuck: the blue path is the longest and easiest to fumble — follow "left → back-lower → middle → pillar left", don't skip a mirror.
