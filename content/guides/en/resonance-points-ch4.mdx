Chapter 4 From Hell It Rose has **17 points**: 3 automatic combat rewards + 14 scattered pickups. This chapter is puzzle-heavy and winding, so scattered points are easy to miss.

|---|---|---|
| 17 | 31 | High |

## ⚠️ Miss Risk

- **Point 1**: behind you at the chapter start — easiest to miss.
- **Points 9-11**: the dead-end before/after Leni squeezes through the wall.
- The 3 combat rewards (2-3, 7-8, 13-14) are automatic.

> 14 of the 17 are scattered, with plenty of puzzle rooms and dead ends — sweep after each puzzle.
