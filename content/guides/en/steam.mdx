## Steam vs Game Pass?

- **Xbox Game Pass subscribers**: the game is **day one** on PC and console Game Pass, with Xbox Play Anywhere (one purchase, console + PC)
- **Steam purchase**: for players who want the achievement system, cards/badges, and long-term offline collection
- **PS5**: the only platform with the DualSense experience (no PS4 version)
- Also on the Epic Games Store

> Note: at launch, Steam's concurrent players matched Requiem's peak — but many players actually play via Game Pass, so the Steam curve doesn't tell the whole story.
