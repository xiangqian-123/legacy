The Broken Spear is the only Hero Tomb blade that requires **no puzzle**, located in Chapter 9.

## Overview

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Chapter | Ch9 Night Has Come |
| How to get | Hero Tomb #5 (entrance already open, no puzzle) |
| Technique | Sprint Assault (sprint attacks pierce shields) |
| Linked achievement | Plunderer / Adaptable |

## Location

At the start of sub-chapter 4 "Hunted":

1. After the cutscene where a soldier runs toward you and is dragged off by the creature
2. **Immediately turn left**, take the left-most path and climb onto the high platform
3. Find the bull pillar, descend the stairs and open the chest in the tomb

> This is the only Hero Tomb with no light-beam puzzle — the entrance is already open, with just two lamps guarding your way in.

## Tips

- **Sprint Assault**: sprint attacks pierce enemy guards and shields, dealing damage.
- **Shield-enemy kryptonite** — switch to this blade when facing shielded foes.
