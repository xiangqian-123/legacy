The Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy soundtrack is by series composer **Olivier Derivière** — the Plague Tale series' regular composer, also behind A Plague Tale: Requiem. The music here is singled out for praise by both critics and the player community.

## OST Basics

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Composer | Olivier Derivière |
| Availability | Live on launch day (August 27, 2026) |
| Channels | Major streaming platforms (Spotify / Apple Music etc.) + Olivier Derivière's official YouTube channel |
| Signature track | The launch-trailer music: a cover of Depeche Mode's "Enjoy the Silence" |

## Player & Critic Reception

- In reviews, the score is listed as another highlight alongside the "dreamlike art"
- Reddit's post-game community: some players cite "story, visuals, music" as their three reasons when naming it a 2026 game-of-the-year candidate
- Series fans generally agree Derivière keeps the series' "classical instruments + dark atmosphere" identity while adding Mediterranean/Minoan world-music elements

## Trailer Music

The official launch trailer (premiered at Gamescom 2026 Opening Night Live) uses an adaptation of Depeche Mode's "Enjoy the Silence," matched to themes of loss, betrayal and destiny — the most widely shared music clip of the launch window.

## Where to Listen

- Official OST: search "Resonance A Plague Tale Legacy" on streaming platforms
- Composer's channel: Olivier Derivière's YouTube channel
- Trailer: [watch the official launch trailer](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ULLFxFm6vvk)
