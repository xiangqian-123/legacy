Sophia is the protagonist of Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy — the first time the series shifts its lens away from Amicia's family to tell the past of this Requiem ally.

## Who She Is

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Identity | Young plunderer |
| Appears in | A Plague Tale: Requiem (ally) → this game (protagonist/origin story) |
| Timeline | 15 years before Requiem |
| Voice | **Anna Demetriou** |
| Hometown | Venice (flees after a failed heist) |

## Her Drive: The Father Mystery

Everything Sophia does circles one core: **the lost treasure and cure her father chased before his death**. His death drives her to Minotaur Island — a mythical place "most people never leave." She carries a stolen **Minoan sphere** (the key to the light-beam puzzles) and gets pulled into the factions' struggle over the island's mysterious treasure.

## Her Resonance with Theseus

As she goes deeper into the island, Sophia experiences Minoan-era flashbacks and realizes that what resonates inside her is the legendary hero **Theseus** — a figure who lived over two thousand years before her. Some flashback segments put you in control of Theseus:

- He has his **own combat rhythm** and his own Focus Attack
- Sophia's skill upgrades **don't carry over** to him
- Players who rely on red-attack parries must re-adapt after the switch

Theseus's story is Sophia's **dark mirror**: the Theseus myth (slaying the Minotaur) is retold here as a darker version, reflecting Sophia's own journey.

## The Macula Connection (mild spoilers)

The title "Resonance" points straight at the core setup: Sophia's connection to the Minoan age, her mysterious link to Ariadne, and the unkillable creature at the island's heart — every thread ultimately leads to **the origin of the Macula**. Community theories hold that Macula-carrier blood runs in Sophia's veins. For the full reveal, see [Ending Explained](/en/guide/ending).

## What Players Think of Her

- The post-game community mostly rates Sophia herself positively, but debates the plausibility of her "experiencing all this yet never mentioning it to Amicia in Requiem"
- Her combat style (kick + parry + throw) is praised as "solid, satisfying"
- The early duo-exploration segments with Leni are among the best-reviewed parts of the game
