The most complex light puzzle in Chapter 10 — four steps.

## Quick Solution

1. **Light the waterfall-cave mirror**: turn the middle wheel clockwise (not the small aiming wheels)
2. **Reflect to the door**: waterfall mirror → mirror behind the cave → scaffold mirror → middle-platform mirror
3. **Reveal the blue icon**: boost Faro up, he shines the light, you stand on the floor plate and use the orb on the green metal to reveal the hidden blue icon (Sophia notes it)
4. **Match 6 symbols**: walk to the stele in the light, use the orb to reveal 6 symbols, move the red/green/blue crystals on the door to match

## How It Works

- Step 2's light path bounces mirror to mirror, finally reaching the three-pedestal door
- In step 3, after Faro shines you, stand on the plate and shine the green metal — only then does the blue icon appear
- In step 4, reveal the 6 symbols at the stele, then slide the door's crystals with the 3 pedestals

> Why you're stuck: tripping on the step-2 reflection order or forgetting to stand on the plate in step 3. Follow 1→2→3→4, don't skip.
