The most complex light puzzle in Chapter 10 — four steps.

## Quick Solution

1. **Light the waterfall-cave mirror**: turn the middle wheel clockwise (not the small aiming wheels)
2. **Reflect to the door**: waterfall mirror → mirror behind the cave → scaffold mirror → middle-platform mirror
3. **Reveal the blue icon**: boost Faro up, he shines the light, you stand on the floor plate and use the orb on the green metal to reveal the hidden blue icon (Sophia notes it)
4. **Match 6 symbols**: walk to the stele in the light, use the orb to reveal 6 symbols, move the red/green/blue crystals on the door to match

## The 3 Crystals — Which Symbol Each One Matches

The door's three rings each carry a gem, and the warriors carved above the door hold the clues:

| Crystal | Door symbol | Ring + pedestal |
|---|---|---|
| Green | Snake / serpent | Inner ring (sword warrior) — left pedestal |
| Red | Upward arrow | Middle ring (bow and quiver) — middle pedestal |
| Blue | "Chair" (combined L + E shape) | Outer ring (spear) — right pedestal |

The three pedestals in front of the door rotate one ring each: **left = inner ring (green), middle = middle ring (red), right = outer ring (blue)**.

## Door Won't Open?

Confirm all six symbols are recorded — the last three come from the cave-lens chain (two lenses nudged left/right) and the Faro boost, so retrace step 2 and 3 if one is missing. Then check the rings read **snake / upward arrow / chair**, and that you're standing in the central light when splitting the beams.

## How It Works

- Step 2's light path bounces mirror to mirror, finally reaching the three-pedestal door
- In step 3, after Faro shines you, stand on the plate and shine the green metal — only then does the blue icon appear
- In step 4, reveal the 6 symbols at the stele, then slide the door's crystals with the 3 pedestals

> Why you're stuck: tripping on the step-2 reflection order or forgetting to stand on the plate in step 3. Follow 1→2→3→4, don't skip.

## FAQ

**Q: How do you solve the Chapter 10 Parallel Paths door puzzle?**
A: Four stages — light the waterfall-cave mirror with the middle wheel, reflect the beam to the door across the three mirrors, boost Faro up and stand on the floor plate to reveal the blue icon, then match the 6 revealed symbols by sliding the crystals with the three pedestals.

**Q: What symbol matches each crystal on the Chapter 10 door?**
A: Green = snake/serpent (inner ring), red = upward arrow (middle ring), blue = "chair" shape (outer ring). The left pedestal rotates the inner ring, the middle pedestal the middle ring, the right pedestal the outer ring.
