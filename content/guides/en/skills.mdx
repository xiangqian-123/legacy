The skill tree's currency is **Resonance Points**. The short version: **unlock Hero's Step (survival) and Aegean Deflect (parry) first — you can respec for free anytime, so don't overthink it.**

## Resonance Point Mechanics

| Item | Notes |
|---|---|
| Source 1 | Glowing **orange shell-like collectibles** in the environment (some on the main path, some in corners) |
| Source 2 | Awarded **automatically** after certain story battles (can't miss) |
| Total | **200 points** in the whole game |
| To max out | **165 points** (35-point buffer) |
| Respec | Free anytime in the Skills menu, no penalty |
| ⚠️ Carry-over | Points **don't carry over via chapter select**; replaying a chapter resets the total to what it was then |

> The biggest trap: points can't be mopped up after the credits like charms. Grab every one you see.

## Five Skills Rated

| Skill | Effect | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| **Aegean Deflect** | Perfect parry stagger damage doubled. Mastery ①: **can parry red attacks** (a game-changer vs Brutes) ② the next sword hit after a parry deals bonus damage | High |
| **Hero's Step** | Double-tap dodge triggers a **second dodge** with i-frames, creating distance to reposition | High (best for beginners) |
| **Echo's Return** | Grab an attacker and **hurl them into nearby enemies** for crowd stagger. The base version only works on normal attacks (yellow/red need mastery) | Medium |
| **Bullbreaker Kick** | The second kick of the combo can push **non-staggered** enemies. Breaks shield enemies + terrain kills (cliffs/hazards) | Medium |
| **Fated Cast** | Store a picked-up throwable **on your belt**, then fire it with R3 later | Low (QoL) |

## Three Build Routes

**Balanced** (recommended for beginners)
Aegean Deflect (red-attack mastery) → Hero's Step → Echo's Return → Bullbreaker Kick / Fated Cast

**Parry build** (aggressive)
Aegean Deflect → Echo's Return → Hero's Step → Bullbreaker Kick

**Dodge build** (safe)
Hero's Step → Echo's Return → Aegean Deflect → Fated Cast

## Skill × Weapon × Charm Synergies

- **Focus build**: Sica (Focus Rush refills focus on kill) + focus-retention charm → spam focus skills
- **Boss build**: Khopesh (Deep Bleed damage over time) against bosses
- **Parry build**: Xiphos (Parry Break, much stronger parry stagger at the cost of no block) + an arm-band charm to widen the parry window
