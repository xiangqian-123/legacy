Focus Entertainment shipped the **October 6 Bug Fix Update** for Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy on 2026-10-06 (SteamDB **Build 25654554**; third-party stores list it as **Update 1.003 / 01.003.000**). The headline fixes: the **Chapter 9 crouch** after palace backtracking, the **Chapter 3 Leni lens** trigger, the **Chapter 13 save blocker**, the **Chapter 14 Book** after credits, and the **Falchion disappearing** after installing DLC.

## Full change summary

### General
- AMD Anti-Lag with Frame Generation support
- 21:9 ultrawide support
- PS5 PSSR setting now persists between sessions

### Performance
- Reduced stutter near the new Nemesis Area
- Fixed Xbox Series X audio stutter

### Combat
- Fixed enemies dying on ledges and edges
- Fixed enemy reaction after a restart
- New skill popup now appears after spending Resonance Points

### Controls
- Remapping for Book navigation, Focus Attack and Prism unlock
- Joystick swap preference now persists

### Chapter fixes
- **Chapter 3** — Leni's lens interaction can now be triggered (see the [Ch3 walkthrough](/en/guide/chapter-3-those-before-us))
- **Chapter 9** — crouch works again in the petrified area after palace backtracking (see the [Ch9 walkthrough](/en/guide/chapter-9-night-has-come))
- **Chapter 13** — removed the progression/save blocker after the corrupted squeeze-through (see the [Ch13 walkthrough](/en/guide/chapter-13-as-one))
- **Chapter 14** — the Book is usable after the credits (see the [Ch14 walkthrough](/en/guide/chapter-14-what-remains))

### Other
- NPC, navigation, collision, localization and language boot fixes

## What is still not confirmed fixed

- **Controller remapping reset** (DualSense Edge) — reported on Steam around Oct 4–5. The patch fixes some remap/joystick persistence, but we can't confirm every case yet.
- **Chapter 9 crash / SteamOS freeze** — a separate issue from the crouch bug; not confirmed fixed.
- **Chapter 7 grapple glitch** and **Chapter 11 crash to desktop** — being re-verified after the patch.

> **A note on stutter.** The official wording for the Nemesis Area is "reduced stutter lag" — **not** "all stuttering fixed". The Game Pass shader-precompilation stutter was handled in an earlier hotfix; the Xbox Series X audio stutter is fixed; other PC frame-pacing may still occur. See the [stuttering fix guide](/en/guide/stuttering).

## Previous updates

- **Shader precompilation stutter (Game Pass)** — fixed in an earlier hotfix. If you still see stutter, update the game and your drivers (see [System Requirements](/en/guide/system-requirements)).
