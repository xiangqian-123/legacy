Chapter 1 Blood Ties is Sophia's childhood prologue. There are **2 collectibles** here, both right along the story path — no puzzles involved.

## Collectible Overview

| Type | Count | Details |
|---|---|---|
| Blade | 1 | Rusty Sword (automatic) |
| Artefact | 1 | Handled Cross |
| Charm | 0 | — |
| Resonance Points | 0 | — |

## Route (4 sub-chapters)

1. **A new home** — training scene; get the Rusty Sword automatically
2. **Faro's cabin** — no collectibles
3. **The search for the key** — pick up the Handled Cross in the courtyard
4. **The fighting pit** — no collectibles

## Collectible Locations

- **Rusty Sword**: obtained automatically during sub-chapter 1 (the training). It's your first weapon, with the technique "Nothing Remarkable".
- **Handled Cross** (Relic of the Present): sub-chapter 3 "The search for the key" — enter the desert courtyard and find it by the corpse on the ground beneath the white branches at the center.

## Chapter Achievement

- **Blood Ties** (complete Chapter 1, 15 points)
