Chapter 11 Patera enters the light factory and the mines. There are **28 collectibles** (no blades).

## Collectible Overview

| Type | Count | Details |
|---|---|---|
| Blade | 0 | — |
| Artefact | 3 | Olden Abacus / Burnt Mosaic / Weasel Foot |
| Charm | 2 | Arm Band #5 / Bracelet #5 |
| Resonance Points | 23 | — |

## Route (6 sub-chapters)

1. **Under the statue** — light-beam puzzle
2. **Prisoners of the factory** — crank puzzle
3. **Puppets** — lens section (collectible-dense)
4. **The last lens** — light-beam puzzle
5. **In the bowels of the mechanism** — giant worm chase
6. **The mines** — wrap-up

## Puzzle Solutions

- **Light-beam puzzle (Puppets)**: use the right-most lever to release it and let Faro take over, then pull the left lever and use the light to melt the dust.
- **Crank puzzle**: push the weight to the lever to make a light shield, pull the lever to swing the light to the second crank, then turn the crank all the way.
- **The last lens**: stand on the elevator and wait for Faro to lift it, crouch past the petrified enemies, burn the dust off the chain with the orb, and turn the crank to the end.

## Artefact Locations

- **Olden Abacus** (Past): after the enemy fight, squeeze through the hole, jump the cliff, turn right (before the ladder).
- **Burnt Mosaic** (Past): after burning the white spikes with light, between the two kilns.
- **Weasel Foot** (Present): the mine-segment camp, above the hammock on the right.

## Charm Locations

- **Arm Band #5**: the "Puppets" segment — past the light, left door, right mirror; after clearing enemies, in the crate by the mirror.
- **Bracelet #5**: after "The last lens" enemy fight, in the crate to the right of the elevator pole at the back of the room.
