**Official estimate: about 20 hours for the main story**, per Focus Entertainment's FAQ — which makes Resonance the longest game in the A Plague Tale series.

## Quick Time Table

| Play style | Estimated time |
|---|---|
| Official estimate (Focus FAQ) | ~20 hours |
| Main story, fast play | ~15 hours (IGN's reviewer took 13 including side exploration) |
| No-commentary critical-path videos | 7h41–8h49, median ~7h54 |
| Main story + casual collecting | 16–20 hours |
| Completionist (30 charms + 8 blades + 200 points) | 20–25 hours+ |
| Platinum / all achievements | ~25 hours |

> Note: the 20-hour figure is the publisher's estimate; actual time varies with difficulty and exploration habits. The 7h41–8h49 range comes from full no-commentary playthroughs and is a critical-path floor — it's not what a normal player exploring and fighting expects to land at.

## Why do guides disagree (8h vs 13h vs 20h)?

You'll see three different numbers for this game, and they're all "true" for different players. Each one measures a different kind of run:

| Number you'll see | What it actually measures | Who lands there |
|---|---|---|
| ~8 hours | No-commentary critical-path videos (7h41–8h49) | Speed-focused runs that skip cutscenes, side routes and optional combat — a floor, not a normal player |
| ~13–15 hours | Reviewer / fast main-story runs | Critics on deadline, playing the story while skipping most side content |
| ~20 hours | Focus Entertainment's official estimate | A normal story-focused player exploring as they go |
| 20–25+ hours | Completionist / platinum | All 30 charms, 8 blades and 200 Resonance Points |

The ~20-hour figure is the publisher's **estimate**, not a measured median. When another guide says "8 hours", it's almost always quoting a full no-commentary video — which skips everything a real player does. When it says "13 hours", that's usually a reviewer rushing to cover the game.

So the honest answer is **about 20 hours** for a normal playthrough — down to ~8h only if you're rushing the critical path, up to 25h+ if you're chasing the platinum.

## Why the Platinum Takes Longer

The three collectible tracks differ in difficulty:

| Track | Retroactive via chapter select? | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Charms ×30 | ✅ Yes | The post-game select screen shows each chapter's count — easy cleanup |
| Blades ×8 | ✅ Yes | 6 hide in Hero Tomb puzzles; redo the puzzle in the chapter |
| Resonance Points ×200 | ⚠️ **No** | Replaying a chapter resets the total to what it was then — grab every one on the first run |

> This is the biggest trap in the achievement design: points can't be mopped up after the fact like charms. See [Collectibles Overview](/en/guide/collectibles).

## Pacing Reference (No Spoilers)

- **Chapters 1–3**: tutorial and setup, slow-paced (community consensus)
- **After Chapter 3**: the combat system and dual timeline fully open up
- **Chapter 6 What Lies Beneath**: the most "true to the series' roots" chapter
- **Chapter 8 Resonance**: a pure-story chapter with no collectibles
- **The back half**: horror elements unfold toward the finale As One

## FAQ

**Q: How long is Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy?**
A: About 20 hours for the main story, per Focus Entertainment's official FAQ — the longest game in the series. Fast players can finish in around 15 hours (IGN's reviewer took 13 with side exploration); completionists should budget 20–25+ hours.

**Q: Is Resonance longer than Requiem?**
A: Yes. The official estimate is about 20 hours, versus roughly 17 hours for Requiem and 11 hours for Innocence.

**Q: How long does it take to 100% Resonance?**
A: Budget 20–25 hours or more. One trap: the 200 Resonance Points are not retroactive via chapter select — replaying a chapter resets your total to what it was then — so missed points can force a second full run.
