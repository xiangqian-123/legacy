Chapter 13 As One is the final chapter, with **8 points** (Chapter 14 has none, folded into this chapter's progress).

| Points this chapter | Running total | Miss risk |
|---|---|---|
| 8 | 193 | Medium |

## Point Locations

| # | Sub-chapter | Location |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 The Labyrinth | After the platform jumps and squeeze, straight ahead around the corner |
| 2 | 1 The Labyrinth | After the 2nd monster escape, on the platform by the breakable lamp |
| 3 | 2 Illusions | After the combat + pedestal gate, jump the gap, on the ground ahead |
| 4 | 2 Illusions | Turn right, down the right stairs, around the corner |
| 5 | 3 Surviving corruption | By the middle mirror in the petrified room (after burning the white spikes) |
| 6 | 4 One last Trial | After the pedestal gate, through the gate, jump the gap, right (before the hole) |
| 7 | 4 One last Trial | Next to the light you turn on in the petrified area |
| 8 | 5 By your side | During the worm chase, after sliding under the door, light on, grapple ×2, right around the corner |

## ⚠️ Miss Risk

- **Point 8**: the corner point during the worm chase.
- Points 5, 7 are in petrified-heavy areas — crouch around them.

> If you haven't missed anything, you'll be at ~193 points here (well past the 165 target). Confirm ≥165 in the Artefacts menu before the credits.
