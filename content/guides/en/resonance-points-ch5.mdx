Chapter 5 Desolation It Wrought has the **most points of any chapter (29)**. The biggest risk is the Four-Symbol Trials — **once you insert a symbol you can't turn back**, so clear each path before moving on.

| Points this chapter | Running total | Miss risk |
|---|---|---|
| 29 | 60 | Very high |

## ⚠️ Miss Risk

- **Points 6, 11**: battlefield detours off the main path.
- **Points 13-14**: backtrack Leni's path on reuniting.
- **The Four-Symbol Trials are the biggest miss zone** — no turning back after inserting a symbol.

> About 6 of the 29 are automatic combat rewards; the other 23 must be found. The most checklist-driven chapter in the game.
