The Xiphos is the last Hero Tomb blade, located in Chapter 10 — the highest-cost and most thrilling technique.

## Overview

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Chapter | Ch10 Parallel Paths |
| How to get | Hero Tomb #6 (light puzzle) |
| Technique | Parry Break (much stronger parry stagger, **cost: cannot block**) |
| Linked achievement | Plunderer / Adaptable |

## Location & Solution

Sub-chapter 2 "In the plunderers' wake", the cave before the mountain:

1. Find the bull statue
2. **First smash the jars in the area** to reveal a blue gem hidden beneath one
3. Use the pedestal to turn on the light, stand on the right-corner light platform and align the three colors (red = left / green = middle / blue = right · hidden under the jar)
4. The tomb in front of the pedestal opens; drop down and open the chest

## Tips

- **Parry Break**: parries deal much stronger stagger damage — built for an aggressive parry style.
- ⚠️ **Cost**: Sophia **cannot block** — you must rely entirely on parrying. A skill-based weapon; avoid if you struggle with timing.
