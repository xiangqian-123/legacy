The short answer: **yes, it has Chinese — with full voice dubbing.** Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy's Simplified Chinese reaches "UI + full voice + subtitles" — a first-tier language in the game's support.

## Simplified Chinese Details

| Dimension | Support |
|---|---|
| UI | ✅ Simplified Chinese |
| Audio | ✅ **Full Simplified-Chinese voice** (complete dubbing) |
| Subtitles | ✅ Simplified Chinese |

> Of the game's 17 languages, **only 6 are fully voiced**: English, French, German, Spanish (Spain), **Simplified Chinese**, and Brazilian Portuguese (per the Steam page). Simplified Chinese making the full-voice tier is unusually generous.

## Traditional Chinese Support

| Dimension | Support |
|---|---|
| UI | ✅ Traditional Chinese |
| Audio | ❌ No Traditional-Chinese dubbing |
