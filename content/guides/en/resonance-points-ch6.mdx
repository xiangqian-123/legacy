Chapter 6 What Lies Beneath is the creature-chase chapter with **22 points** — 2 of them hide along the chase route and are very hard to grab while fleeing.

| Points this chapter | Running total | Miss risk |
|---|---|---|
| 22 | 82 | High |

## ⚠️ Miss Risk

- **Points 8, 9 (chase)**: the hardest 2 points in the game — miss them and you redo the segment (points don't carry over).
- **Points 6-7**: turn right off the main path after the grapple climb.
- **Points 20-21**: on the hilltop during the chase, grab fast.

> No automatic combat rewards this chapter — all 22 must be found. Memorize the chase points and grab them on a retry.
