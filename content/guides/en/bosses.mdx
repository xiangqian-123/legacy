The game's "bosses" come in two kinds: the one true boss fight (the final Minotaur), and the special-mechanic elite enemies that recur throughout (Creature / Brute / Corrupted / Petrified). This page breaks down each type.

## Final Boss: Minotaur (Chapter 13)

The final fight at the end of Chapter 13 As One is a full-mechanics check. Three core moves:

1. **Dual-timeline "Resonance" reshapes the arena** — switch between the medieval and Minoan timelines to alter the arena structure and create positioning/paths
2. **The light orb exposes the weakness** — use it to reveal the boss's opening and land damage in the window
3. **Precise parries keep you alive** — the boss hits hard, so you must rely on well-timed parries

**Recommended loadout**:
- **Khopesh** (Deep Bleed damage over time) — the most reliable for bosses
- **Xiphos** (Parry Break, much stronger parry stagger) — aggressive parry build, but no block and low margin for error

> The hard part is juggling multiple mechanics at once: dodging the boss, switching timelines to reshape the arena, and finding light-orb windows. Get comfortable with the parry rhythm first.

## The Creature (recurring monster)

An unkillable presence that appears repeatedly from Chapter 6 on — you can only **flee, hide and go around**, never kill it:

- **Light is protection**: lamps, light pillars and light orbs are your safe zone — advance along the light
- **Stay crouched to sneak**: while crouching it won't chase you, so you can search for nearby collectibles freely
- **Run the right route when chased**: follow the blue floor lines / light-point hints, don't flail around
- Linked achievements: Close Call (5 narrow escapes), Unpredictable (ditch it across gaps 25 times)

## Brute (elite heavy)

- **Red attacks can't be blocked at first** — dodge only
- Unlocking **Aegean Deflect mastery** lets you parry red attacks — the game-changer for parry builds against Brutes
- **They hit soldiers when enraged**: use this for the Cannon Fodder achievement (bait a Brute into killing a soldier while enraged)

## Corrupted

- They **grab Sophia** — break free with a **Focus Attack** when grabbed (linked to the Excessive Force achievement)
- Keep your focus gauge stocked and release it the moment you're grabbed

## Petrified

- Weak to **light**: shining light destroys them (linked to the They Were People achievement, 100 cumulative)
- They wake when you get close — **stay crouched and go around** in stealth sections, or handle them from range with light

## Presences (ancient warriors, Chapter 13)

The Minoan ancient warriors in Chapter 13 interact with the light-orb and dual-timeline mechanics. The Kopis's combo stagger works wonders against them.
