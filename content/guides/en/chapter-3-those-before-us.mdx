Chapter 3 Those Before Us moves from the paradise beach into the Minoan ruins. There are **19 collectibles**, **1 puzzle** and the easily missed "Sea You Later" turtle achievement.

## Route (5 sub-chapters)

1. **Paradise** — beach + forest, the densest stretch. Spawn on the beach: turn around for the turtle achievement, and check the wrecked boat (Resonance Point). The Conch Shell sits in the seaside cave; after the green door, swing across the gap for the Play Onyx. Boost Leni onto the ledge, push the log, and the Golden Cup waits at the far beach's dead end.
2. **Vestiges** — the large statue area. Jump the gap to the ruins for the Heroic Plaque; grapnel down the fence and go up the right stairs for the Strategy Game (Arm Band #1 drops through the broken floor below it).
3. **Rats in a Barrel** — inside the buildings: Beads #2 at the bottom after sliding in; Bracelet #2 in the left room where Leni needs help with the door.
4. **On the Run** — Seal #1, after reuniting with Leni and sliding down, past the fence.
5. **The start of the trials** — cross the river into the temple and solve the lens platform puzzle to descend.

## ⚠️ Missable Achievement: Sea You Later

Right after spawning on the beach, **turn around** and walk along the shoreline until you see the turtle; wait for the dialogue to end and the achievement pops. The main path faces the opposite way, so it's easy to walk straight past.

## Puzzle: Three-Latch Platform (end of the chapter)

The temple platform is held by **three latches**. Chain light through the lenses onto **three golden dials** to release them:

1. Interact with the sphere pedestal in the centre to activate the light.
2. **Latch 1**: use the lens on the right to shine light straight onto the golden dial ahead.
3. **Latch 2**: rotate the same lens left so the beam hits the lens on the platform left of the pedestal — walk over to that lens and aim it at the dial on the left wall.
4. **Latch 3**: in the northeast corner, find the **broken lens** on the ground and let Leni hold it up; return to the previous lens and shine into it — the beam bounces onto the final dial.
5. Insert the sphere into the centre pedestal — the platform descends and the chapter ends.

## Artefact Locations

- **Conch Shell** (Present): beach — climb into the cave to the right of the big green door.
- **Play Onyx** (Past): after the green door, climb the rock and swing over the gap to the forest; a shiny coin in the large niche on the left.
- **Golden Cup** (Past): after pushing the log, squeeze through the gap to the other beach, walk to the dead end at the forest's edge.
- **Heroic Plaque** (Past): after jumping the gap to the ruins, on the ground of the nearest small ruin by the cliff.
- **Strategy Game** (Past): after the grapnel pulls down the fence, in the big statue area — up the right stairs, jump left at the top.
- **Minoan King** (Past): after Leni lifts the log and you crawl the tunnel, in the ruins behind the mural at the exit.

## Charm Locations

- **Beads #2**: after sliding into the ruins, go to the bottom and check the crate on the left.
- **Arm Band #1**: in the big statue area, drop through the broken floor beneath the Strategy Game.
- **Bracelet #2**: in the next building, the crate in the left room where Leni asks for help with the door.
- **Seal #1**: after reuniting with Leni and sliding down, past the fence, take the next left to a small crate.

## FAQ

**Q: How do you solve the Chapter 3 lens puzzle?**
A: Activate the sphere pedestal in the centre, then chain light through the lenses onto three golden dials — dial 1 straight ahead from the right lens, dial 2 by rotating that lens left into the platform lens and aiming at the left wall, dial 3 by shining into the broken lens (Leni holds it) in the northeast corner. Insert the sphere and the platform descends.

**Q: Is the Sea You Later turtle achievement missable?**
A: Yes. Right after spawning on the beach, turn around and walk along the shoreline to the turtle; wait for the dialogue to end and the achievement pops. The main path faces the other way, so it's easy to walk straight past.

**Q: How many collectibles are in Chapter 3?**
A: 19 — 6 artefacts, 4 charms and 9 Resonance Points. No blade in this chapter.
