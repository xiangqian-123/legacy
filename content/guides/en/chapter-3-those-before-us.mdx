Chapter 3 Those Before Us moves from the paradise beach into the Minoan ruins. There are **19 collectibles**, including the easily missed "Sea You Later" turtle achievement.

## Collectible Overview

| Type | Count | Details |
|---|---|---|
| Blade | 0 | — |
| Artefact | 6 | Conch Shell / Play Onyx / Golden Cup / Heroic Plaque / Strategy Game / Minoan King |
| Charm | 4 | Beads #2 / Arm Band #1 / Bracelet #2 / Seal #1 |
| Resonance Points | 9 | — |

## Route (5 sub-chapters)

1. **Paradise** — beach + forest, densest (the turtle achievement is here)
2. **Vestiges** — large statue area
3. **Rats in a Barrel** — inside buildings
4. **On the Run** — Seal #1 charm
5. **The start of the trials** — cross the river into the temple

## ⚠️ Missable Achievement: Sea You Later

Right after spawning on the beach, **turn around** and walk along the shoreline until you see the turtle; wait for the dialogue to end and the achievement pops. The main path faces the opposite way, so it's easy to walk straight past.

## Artefact Locations

- **Conch Shell** (Present): beach — climb into the cave to the right of the big green door.
- **Play Onyx** (Past): after the green door, climb the rock and swing over the gap to the forest; a shiny coin in the large niche on the left.
- **Golden Cup** (Past): after pushing the log, squeeze through the gap to the other beach, walk to the dead end at the forest's edge.
- **Heroic Plaque** (Past): after jumping the gap to the ruins, on the ground of the nearest small ruin by the cliff.
- **Strategy Game** (Past): after the grapnel pulls down the fence, in the big statue area — up the right stairs, jump left at the top.
- **Minoan King** (Past): after Leni lifts the log and you crawl the tunnel, in the ruins behind the mural at the exit.

## Charm Locations

- **Beads #2**: after sliding into the ruins, go to the bottom and check the crate on the left.
- **Arm Band #1**: in the big statue area, drop through the broken floor beneath the Strategy Game.
- **Bracelet #2**: in the next building, the crate in the left room where Leni asks for help with the door.
- **Seal #1**: after reuniting with Leni and sliding down, past the fence, take the next left to a small crate.

## Resonance Points (9) — Quick Notes

Clustered in "Paradise" beach/forest (5), the "Vestiges" ruin area (3) and the temple (1). Most sit beside the main path or on high platforms — watch out while climbing.

## Chapter Achievements

- Those Before Us (complete Chapter 3)
- Sea You Later (see the turtle)
