Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy's critical reception is in: **Metascore 78** (Generally Favorable, across 59 critics), **user score 8.1** (274 ratings). This page rounds up the critics' highlights and the community's real feedback.

## Score Overview

| Dimension | Data |
|---|---|
| Metascore | **78** / 100 (59 critics) |
| Critic split | 76% positive (45) / 24% mixed (14) / 0% negative |
| User score | **8.1** / 10 (274 ratings) |
| Critic range | 60 – 95 |

## What Critics Say

**Praised**:

- **Visuals & art**: nearly every outlet calls it the series' best-looking game, repeatedly praising the Mediterranean coastline and the underground maze art design
- **Puzzle design**: the light-reflection puzzles are widely considered the game's biggest highlight — the prism-geometry puzzles plus the ancient "flashlight"-style tool feel fresh
- **Setting**: the parallel Minoan-era narrative and the myth/story intertextuality draw praise

**Criticized**:

- **Combat depth**: several outlets call the combat "shallow" and "mechanical" — the game's weakest link
- **Story pacing**: a prequel's inherent problem — players already know Sophia survives into Requiem, so making the story feel weighty is hard; some outlets find it flat

**Representative quotes** (from Metacritic):

> "…leans into action and melee combat, departing from the formula of its predecessors… Its greatest strength lies in its spectacular setting." (85 range)

> "A sincere effort worth picking up in a slow season, rather than a day-one blind buy." (77 range)

> "…trying very hard to replicate Naughty Dog's Uncharted magic… dragged down by uninspired, irritating combat." (70 range)

## What the Community Says (Reddit)

First-week Reddit discussion shows the classic "**players rate it higher than critics**" pattern:

- **Optimization is top-tier**: "insanely good optimization" is a recurring phrase; OLED players call it the best-looking game of their lives, with ~80 FPS measured on a 120Hz display
- **The gameplay shift divides opinion**: fans say "went in with zero expectations, one of my favorites of the year" (548-upvote post); detractors say "closer to Tomb Raider than Plague Tale"
- **Story debate**: the unexplained Sophia-Ariadne connection and the Creature's overuse are the two most-discussed points among players who finished
- **Meme**: "There is only 1 rat" — players' self-deprecating joke after the series' rat swarms were replaced

> An interesting data point: Steam's concurrent peak matched Requiem's, but the community consensus is that many players actually played via Game Pass — sales shouldn't be read from Steam alone.
