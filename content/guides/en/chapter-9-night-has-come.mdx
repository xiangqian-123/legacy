Chapter 9 Night Has Come enters the creature-occupied night battle and the Minoan palace. There are **30 collectibles**.

> Note: this chapter's **name** is "Night Has Come", but its **achievement** is "Light Will Save Us" — don't confuse the two.

## Collectible Overview

| Type | Count | Details |
|---|---|---|
| Blade | 1 | Broken Spear |
| Artefact | 3 | Dante's Inferno Canto V / Phosphate Ring / Bronze Mirror |
| Charm | 2 | Seal #4 / Pierced Coin #4 |
| Resonance Points | 25 | — |

## Route (6 sub-chapters)

1. **You have to live** — no collectibles
2. **Hell on earth** — escape along the lamps
3. **No respite** — three-way cave
4. **Hunted** — Broken Spear + stealth
5. **Chaos** — zipline combat
6. **Echoes of the past** — Minoan palace

## Blade Location

- **Broken Spear**: at the start of the "Hunted" sub-chapter, after seeing the soldier dragged off by the creature, **immediately turn left** and climb the rock, find the bull pillar, descend and open the chest. Technique "Sprint Assault": sprint attacks pierce shields, a hard counter to shield enemies.

## Artefact Locations

- **Dante's Inferno Canto V** (Present): the three-way cave in "No respite", take the right path.
- **Phosphate Ring** (Past): in the "Hunted" area, at the end of the stairs, crouch through the grass-covered door before the lamp (not the R3-prompt door).
- **Bronze Mirror** (Past): the palace segment, after jumping the cliff and turning on the light, turn right and climb.

## Charm Locations

- **Seal #4**: the "No respite" three-way, left path (squeeze through), in the crate.
- **Pierced Coin #4**: the palace balcony, turn left and climb the wall.

## Stealth Reminder

The "Hunted" segment has 3 Resonance Points scattered through the ruins. **Stay crouched** and the creature won't chase you, so you can search freely.

## Chapter Achievement

- Light Will Save Us (complete Chapter 9 — this is the achievement name)
