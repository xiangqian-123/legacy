The one-line answer: **unlock Hero's Step (survival) and Aegean Deflect (parry) first — everything else is flexible, and respec is free anytime.**

## Beginner Priority Order (Follow This)

| Order | Skill | Why first |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Hero's Step** | Double-dodge with i-frames — a survival game-changer, doubles your margin for error |
| 2 | **Aegean Deflect** | Perfect-parry stagger doubled; mastery lets you **parry red attacks**, a game-changer vs Brutes |
| 3 | **Echo's Return** | Hurl an enemy into a crowd for group stagger, efficient mob clearing |
| 4 | **Bullbreaker Kick** | Kicks push non-staggered enemies — cliff/terrain kills against shield users |
| 5 | **Fated Cast** | Pure QoL — unlock last, not needed to finish |

## Why This Order

- **Survival first**: combat intensity is high from Chapter 3, and Hero's Step's i-frames are the most direct survival tool
- **Parry is the core loop**: the combat is "parry for stagger → kick/combo for damage", and Aegean Deflect is the foundation
- **Red-attack parry is the watershed**: before Aegean Deflect mastery you can only dodge Brute red attacks; after it you can counter — a night-and-day difference
- **The last two are icing**: Echo's Return and Bullbreaker Kick are clear-speed, Fated Cast is pure convenience

## Are There Enough Points?

Maxing all 6 branches needs **165 points** (200 in the game, a 35-point buffer). A normal first run grabbing points as you go comfortably hits 165 before the credits.

> ⚠️ The one catch: Resonance Points **don't carry over via chapter select** — grab every one you see. See the [Resonance Points guide](/en/guide/resonance-points).
