Chapter 12 Fading Light crosses the light bridge and the quarry. There are **11 collectibles** (no blades).

## Collectible Overview

| Type | Count | Details |
|---|---|---|
| Blade | 0 | — |
| Artefact | 2 | Loving Memento / Blank Mosaic |
| Charm | 1 | Seal #5 |
| Resonance Points | 8 | — |

## Route (4 sub-chapters)

1. **The beam** — bridge section (collectible-dense)
2. **The wrecked quarry** — creature chase
3. **The creature's fury** — escape
4. **A few more steps** — the third trial

## Artefact Locations

- **Loving Memento** (Present): the large open area after the enemy fight, turn right by the corpse.
- **Blank Mosaic** (Past): at the start of the third trial, after the grapnel swing over the gap, turn right and jump the gap.

## Charm Location

- **Seal #5**: after the fight, slide down the slope and turn right to the corner crate.

## Resonance Point Reminder

Most of this chapter's Resonance Points sit beside the main path (8 total). Watch especially for the one after smashing the lamps in "The beam" and the one to the right before the crawl-hole in the quarry.

## Chapter Achievement

- Fading Light (complete Chapter 12)
