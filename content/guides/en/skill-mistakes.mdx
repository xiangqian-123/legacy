The biggest skill-tree trap isn't a "wrong pick" — it's the Resonance Point mechanics. Wrong picks are free to respec; **missed points can't be recovered**.

## Pitfall 1: Points Don't Carry Over via Chapter Select (the deadly one)

Resonance Points **don't carry over through chapter select**. Replaying an earlier chapter resets your total to what it was when you left it.

- ❌ Wrong: thinking "I'll top up points post-game via chapter select"
- ✅ Right: grab every point on the first run, targeting 165

**This is the only permanently missable element** — miss 165 and "Like A Warrior" requires a fresh save. See the [Resonance Points guide](/en/guide/resonance-points).

## Pitfall 2: Not Re-Grabbing Points After Dying

Dying **loses the points collected since the last checkpoint**. If you grabbed 2 points and died before saving, they're gone.

- ✅ Right: on a retry, re-collect that segment's points before pushing on

## Pitfall 3: Parrying Red Attacks Without Aegean Deflect Mastery

Brute red attacks **can't be blocked at first** — dodge only. Many players charge in without Aegean Deflect mastery and get wrecked.

- ✅ Right: unlock **Aegean Deflect mastery** first, then parry red attacks — the Brute game-changer

## Pitfall 4: Prioritizing Fated Cast

Fated Cast (store a throwable on your belt) is pure QoL — it barely affects combat power. Its "destined throw" name sounds cool, but it wastes points early.

- ✅ Right: unlock Fated Cast **last** — the first four (Hero's Step / Aegean Deflect / Echo's Return / Bullbreaker Kick) are what matter in combat

## Pitfall 5: Fighting Theseus Flashbacks with Sophia's Muscle Memory

In the Theseus flashback segments, **Sophia's skill upgrades don't carry over** — especially red-attack parry, which becomes unavailable.

- ✅ Right: in flashbacks, **dodge to learn the rhythm first**, don't fight like your parry build

## One-Line Summary

> Wrong picks don't hurt (free respec) — **missed points are fatal** (no carry-over). Grab every one, unlock survival and parry first.
