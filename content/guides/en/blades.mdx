There are **8 blades** in the game (including the starting Rusty Sword), each with a unique combat technique. Two are obtained automatically from the story, and **6 hide in Hero Tomb light puzzles**. This page covers every location.

## All Blades

| Blade | Chapter | Technique | Best use | How to get |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Rusty Sword](/en/guide/rusty-sword) | Ch1 | None | Only for the Adaptable trophy | Starting weapon, obtained automatically entering the desert village |
| [Falchion](/en/guide/falchion) | Ch2 | Momentum Strike: bonus damage after a crit | General combat, crit build | Story (the armory by the carnival water, together with the grapnel) |
| [Kopis](/en/guide/kopis) | Ch4 | Stagger Blow: big stagger on the final combo hit | Crowd control, stagger build | Cave main path — interact with the skeleton-statue pedestal to open the floor, chest down the stairs (almost impossible to miss) |
| [Greek Sword](/en/guide/greek-sword) | Ch5 | Spinning Slash: crits become a 360° whirlwind | 3+ enemy fights | Four-symbol central hub — path under the serrated symbol → clear enemies → around the corner by the green door → camp skeleton pillar |
| [Sica](/en/guide/sica) | Ch6 | Focus Rush: kills refill the focus gauge | Focus-skill spam build | After the Daedalus key opens the door, left side-tunnel → skeleton-statue hall |
| [Khopesh](/en/guide/khopesh) | Ch7 | Deep Bleed: final combo hit applies damage over time | **Boss fights**, sustained damage | Underground storage — after the grapnel collapses the ceiling, up the stairs, turn left |
| [Broken Spear](/en/guide/broken-spear) | Ch9 | Sprint Assault: sprint attacks pierce shields | Shield-enemy counter | Immediately turn left after the soldier is petrified in the cutscene (**no light puzzle, entrance already open**) |
| [Xiphos](/en/guide/xiphos) | Ch10 | Parry Break: much stronger parry stagger, **cost: cannot block** | Aggressive parry build | Across from the rotating-gem puzzle door — cave behind tall reeds → grapnel point → the last skeleton statue |

## Hero Tomb Light-Puzzle Method

Five blades hide in Hero Tombs, all sharing the same routine:

1. Find the **green gem** — always on the main statue/pillar base
2. Find the marked **standing platform / metal ring** on the ground — that's basically your designated spot
3. Lock the base beam onto the green gem first, then rotate to align the **red/blue beams** to their targets
4. All three lit → the floor/tomb door opens

> Xiphos gotcha: smash the jars first — a gem point hides behind one.

## Linked Trophies

| Trophy | Tier | Requirement |
|---|---|---|
| **Plunderer** | Gold | Collect all 8 blades |
| **Adaptable** | Silver | At least 1 kill with every blade (**including the starting Rusty Sword**); the DLC Hero's Blade isn't needed |

> Tip: score one kill immediately after getting each new blade to avoid redoing Adaptable later. The DLC Hero's Blade (Heritage Pack) doesn't count toward any trophy.
