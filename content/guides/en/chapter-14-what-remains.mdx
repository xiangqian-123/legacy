Chapter 14 What Remains is the epilogue — **no collectibles and no standalone achievement**, a pure story wrap-up. Below: the single sub-chapter, what to do after the credits, and the chapter-select cleanup.

If you're here for the ending itself rather than the chapter route, the [Ending Explained](/en/guide/ending) page covers what happens and what it means.

## Sub-chapter

- **Ashes** — the ending cutscene

## Notes

After the credits, return to the main menu and use **chapter select** to collect any blades, charms or artefacts you missed (Resonance Points excluded). With everything collected, you can go for the full achievement/platinum run.

> **Fixed Oct 6:** a bug where the Book was unusable after the credits was fixed in the [October 6 Bug Fix Update](/en/guide/patch-notes) — update your game if the Book won't open after the ending.
