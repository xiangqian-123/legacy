**No, Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy does not have New Game Plus.** As of October 2026 there is no NG+, no DLC expansion, and no roadmap for one.

## Quick Answer

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Is there New Game Plus? | **No** |
| What do you get after the credits? | **Chapter Select** |
| Do collectibles carry over? | Charms, blades, artefacts, echoes — yes; Resonance Points — **no** |
| Last checked | 2026-10-05 |

## What Chapter Select carries over (and what it doesn't)

Finishing the story unlocks **Chapter Select**, which is how you finish the platinum — but it isn't a New Game Plus run:

- **Carries over** — charms, blades, artefacts and Theseus Echoes can all be collected post-game via Chapter Select; the select screen shows each chapter's count.
- **Does not carry over** — Resonance Points. Replaying an older chapter resets your total to what it was when you left that chapter, so you can't farm or top them up later.

> If you care about the **Like A Warrior** achievement (unlock all 6 mastery branches), you must reach 165 Resonance Points in a single playthrough — see the [Resonance Points guide](/en/guide/resonance-points).
