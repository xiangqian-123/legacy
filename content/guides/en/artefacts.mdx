There are **37 artefacts** in the game, split into two types: **Relic of the Present** (medieval timeline) and **Relic of the Past** (Minoan era). Collecting them all unlocks the **Hoarder** achievement (80G).

All of them **can be collected via chapter select** — the select screen shows each chapter's count, and the artefact menu lists them by name in order, making it easy to find gaps. Chapter 8 has no artefacts.
