The skull-door puzzle in Chapter 7's Black Temple.

## Quick Solution

1. Shine the orb at the door to reveal green-dot hints
2. Press: **rightmost skull → 2nd from right → leftmost → 2nd from left**

## How It Works

- Shining the door makes the skulls show green dots — the order hint
- The order is fixed: rightmost → 2nd from right → leftmost → 2nd from left, one press each
- A wrong press resets it — just press again

> Why you're stuck: pressing skulls by feel without first shining the door. Shine → read the green dots → press in order.

## Linked Puzzle

Right after this door is the **Black Temple light puzzle** (shine the wall to find the blue-icon skull, move the light, rotate the base mirror to center, green=center/red=left/blue=right).
