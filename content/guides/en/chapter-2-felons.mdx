Chapter 2 Felons drops you into a Venetian carnival. There are **10 collectibles**, plus an easily missed scene achievement, "Theater Enthusiast".

## Collectible Overview

| Type | Count | Details |
|---|---|---|
| Blade | 1 | Falchion (story) + pre-order Hero's Blade |
| Artefact | 2 | Serenissima Banner / Bombasin Mask |
| Charm | 2 | Beads #1 / Bracelet #1 |
| Resonance Points | 5 | Story reward |

## Route (5 sub-chapters)

1. **Infiltration** — no collectibles
2. **The carnival** — densest (2 artefacts + 2 charms + Falchion + puppet-show achievement)
3. **Rats in a barrel** — Bracelet #1 charm
4. **On the run** — 5 Resonance Points (automatic)
5. **Inferno** — no collectibles

## Collectible Locations

- **Falchion**: by the water in the carnival — interact with the barrel by the boat (this also unlocks the grapnel). The pre-order Heritage Pack adds the Hero's Blade (doesn't count toward trophies).
- **Serenissima Banner** (Relic of the Present): before the bridge, turn right to the dock.
- **Bombasin Mask** (Relic of the Present): in the large carnival square, in the right-hand corner on the ground.
- **Beads #1** charm: after Leni picks the lock, past the two-pig backyard, squeeze through the gap and immediately turn left to a small crate.
- **Bracelet #1** charm: after the grapnel tutorial pulls down the archer, swing over the gap and immediately turn left to a small crate.

## ⚠️ Missable Achievement: Theater Enthusiast

Behind the torch-juggling stage in the carnival square, a small path leads to a puppet show. Stand in front of it for about a minute until the achievement pops — leaving early won't count.

## Chapter Achievements

- Felons (complete Chapter 2)
- Theater Enthusiast (watch the puppet show)
