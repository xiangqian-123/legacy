The Rusty Sword is Sophia's **starting weapon**, obtained automatically during the Chapter 1 Blood Ties training — no searching required.

## Overview

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Chapter | Ch1 Blood Ties |
| How to get | Automatic (starting weapon) |
| Technique | Nothing Remarkable |
| Linked achievement | Adaptable (at least 1 kill per blade) |

## Location

Acquired automatically in the training scene of sub-chapter 1 "A new home". It's the first weapon in the game.

## Tips

- The technique "Nothing Remarkable" has no bonus — it's the baseline every other blade is measured against.
- **Its only real use**: the Adaptable achievement (at least 1 kill with every blade) — the Rusty Sword counts too, so don't forget to score one kill with it.
