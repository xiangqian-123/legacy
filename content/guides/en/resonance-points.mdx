Resonance Points are the **most easily missable** collectible in the game — unlike Blades, Charms, and Artefacts, you **cannot mop them up after finishing the story**.

## The three key numbers

| Total | Required | Buffer |
|---|---|---|
| **200** | **165** | 35 (safe to miss 35) |

> **In short**: collect at least 165 points in a single playthrough. Missing a few is fine, but you can **never** come back for them via Chapter Select.

## Why can't you get them later?

Chapter Select **does not carry over Resonance Points**:

- Replaying an older chapter resets your total to what you had when you left that chapter — later progress is lost
- After dying, points collected since the last checkpoint are lost and must be re-collected
- The Chapter Select screen has no point tracker; only the Artefacts menu (bottom-left) shows your running total

So Resonance Points must be collected **as you play**, in one playthrough.

> Ch5 (29), Ch9 (25), Ch10 (25), and Ch11 (23) are the densest chapters — search these most carefully.

## Best strategy

1. **First playthrough**: grab every point you see; get 1 kill with each new blade (Adaptable achievement)
2. Combat-reward points are automatic — don't miss them
3. After dying, re-collect that section's points before pushing on
4. Before the credits, confirm 165+ points in the Artefacts menu
