Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy is an action-adventure game developed by Asobo Studio and published by Focus Entertainment, released on August 27, 2026 for PS5, Xbox Series X|S and PC (Steam / Epic), day one on Xbox Game Pass. It's the third game in the Plague Tale series and the prequel to A Plague Tale: Requiem — set 15 years earlier.

## Story Background

You play as Sophia, a young plunderer searching for the truth of her past, chasing the lost treasure her father sought before his death, arriving at **Minotaur Island** — a mysterious island in the Sea of Crete riddled with deadly trials, where multiple enemy factions converge on its mysterious treasure, and an unkillable presence deep within hunts every visitor.

As she explores, Sophia experiences **Minoan-era** flashbacks — what resonates inside her is the legendary hero **Theseus**, who lived over two thousand years ago. Some flashback segments let you control Theseus directly in large battles (note: Sophia's skill upgrades don't carry over to him).

The endpoint of both timelines is the very origin of the Macula.

## Gameplay Shift: What's Different from the First Two

- **Combat**: dynamic melee replaces stealth-heavy encounters. Kicks, parries, dodges and throwables build a layered combat system, with noticeably higher intensity from the start than A Plague Tale: Innocence
- **Puzzles**: light-reflection puzzles fully evolved — prism geometry puzzles plus a stolen Minoan sphere as the light-beam key
- **Collectibles**: four tracks — 30 charms, 8 blades, 37 artefacts, 200 Resonance Points
- **Progression**: a skill tree (6 branches + 5 active skills) with free respec anytime

> Tip: the story is fairly self-contained, so you can jump in without playing the first two — but the lore ties directly into the series, so veterans will catch more easter eggs.

## Critical & Player Reception

- Metascore **78** (Generally Favorable, 59 critics): 76% positive / 24% mixed / 0% negative
- Metacritic user score **8.1** (274 ratings)
- The community broadly considers the **optimization excellent** and the visuals the series' best; the main debates center on story pacing and combat depth
- Steam concurrent peak matched Requiem's; the game leaked 7 days before release
