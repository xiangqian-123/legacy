Chapter 13 As One is the final chapter. You cross the labyrinth to face the Minotaur. There are **11 collectibles** (no blades).

## Collectible Overview

| Type | Count | Details |
|---|---|---|
| Blade | 0 | — |
| Artefact | 2 | Labyrinth Plan / Poisoned Syringe |
| Charm | 1 | Trinket #5 |
| Resonance Points | 8 | — |

## Route (6 sub-chapters)

1. **The Labyrinth** — creature chase (3 escapes)
2. **Illusions** — doors and staircases
3. **Surviving corruption** — petrified-enemy room
4. **One last Trial** — petrified enemies
5. **By your side** — giant-worm chase + final charm
6. **The Minotaur** — final boss (no collectibles)

## Artefact Locations

- **Labyrinth Plan** (Past): after the 3rd grapnel-swing escape in the labyrinth, turn left into the dead end before the lamp.
- **Poisoned Syringe** (Present): the petrified-enemy area — squeeze through, then immediately turn left around the corner into a dead end.

## Charm Location

- **Trinket #5**: the "By your side" segment, after the giant-worm chase, up the stairs by the big lamp. **If you haven't missed a single charm, the Superstitious achievement should pop here.**

## Minotaur Final Boss Tips

At the end of Chapter 13 you face the Minotaur: use the dual-timeline "Resonance" ability to reshape the arena, expose the boss's weakness with the light orb, and land precise parries. This fight checks every mechanic — bring the Khopesh (damage over time) or the Xiphos (parry build).

## Chapter Achievements

- As One (complete Chapter 13)
- A New Beginning (complete all chapters)
- Superstitious (if all charms are collected)
