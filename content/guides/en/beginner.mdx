This game's pacing is completely different from the first two: stealth is no longer the focus, and the combat intensity is noticeably higher from the start. This guide compresses the essentials into one line: survive first, get stronger, then collect everything.

## First Things First: This Is an Action Game

- **Combat**: the Kick is an underrated godsend — the second kick can push non-staggered enemies, and near cliffs or hazards it's an instant kill
- **Parry**: yellow attacks can be parried, red attacks must be dodged at first (red becomes parryable after Aegean Deflect mastery)
- **Focus Attack**: unlocked automatically, no skill points. Attacks/parries/dodges build the Focus Gauge; when full, unleash a wide sweep — it pays off most when surrounded, don't save it for bosses
- The "hug the edge and sneak" style of the first two games doesn't work here — head-on combat is the designed main course

## Skills: Unlock These Two First

The skill currency is **Resonance Points** (glowing orange shell-like collectibles + some automatic story-battle rewards). There are 200 points in total; maxing every skill needs only 165, so there's plenty of buffer.

Beginner priority:

1. **Hero's Step**: double-tap dodge for a second dodge with i-frames — a survival game-changer (best for beginners)
2. **Aegean Deflect**: perfect-parry stagger damage doubled; mastery lets you parry red attacks — a game-changer vs Brutes

> Tip: you can **respec for free** anytime in the Skills menu, so don't stress about mistakes.

⚠️ Two traps to know up front:
- Points **don't carry over via chapter select**; replaying an earlier chapter resets the total to what it was then
- Dying loses the points you collected since the last checkpoint

## Puzzles: The General Light-Puzzle Method

Every Hero Tomb light puzzle in the game shares the same routine:

1. Find the **green gem** — always on the main statue or pillar base
2. Find the marked **standing platform / metal ring** on the ground — basically your designated spot
3. Lock the base beam onto the green gem first, then rotate to align the red/blue beams to their targets
4. All three lit → the tomb door opens

## Collecting: When to Get What

| Collectible | Count | Advice |
|---|---|---|
| Charms | 30 | Chapter select exists, **all collectible retroactively** — clean up after the credits |
| Blades | 8 | 2 from the story, 6 in Hero Tomb puzzles; score 1 kill with each new blade immediately (Adaptable needs 1 kill per blade, including the Rusty Sword) |
| Resonance Points | 200 | **Cannot** be topped up via chapter select — grab every one you see, don't save them for later |

Chapter 1 Blood Ties and Chapter 8 Resonance have **no collectibles at all** — just play through.

## Three Opening Tips

1. **Don't hoard your focus gauge**: use it when surrounded — Focus Attack recharges fast
2. **Drop the parry muscle memory when characters switch**: in Theseus flashback fights, Sophia's upgrades don't carry over and red-attack parry is unavailable — dodge first to learn the rhythm
3. **Take the first 3 chapters slow**: the community consensus is that the opening is slow-paced, and combat/story only fully open up after Chapter 3 — give it a little patience
