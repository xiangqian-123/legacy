The Kopis is the first blade obtained from a **Hero Tomb**, located in Chapter 4 — almost impossible to miss.

## Overview

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Chapter | Ch4 From Hell It Rose |
| How to get | Hero Tomb #1 (almost impossible to miss) |
| Technique | Stagger Blow (big stagger on the final combo hit) |
| Linked achievement | Plunderer / Adaptable |

## Location

Sub-chapter 3 "Puzzles and traps" — after the spike-trap room, interact with the pedestal to open the tomb, go down the stairs and open the large chest. It's right on the main path, so you're unlikely to miss it.

## Tips

- The final combo hit causes a big stagger — great for crowd control and stagger builds.
- Works well for players who commit to full combos, especially against heavy/elite enemies.
