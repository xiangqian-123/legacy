Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy has **36 achievements** (1,000 Gamerscore on Xbox; 37 trophies on PlayStation including 1 platinum). The difficulty is low — the platinum takes about 20 hours in a single playthrough. The only real gotcha is that **Resonance Points can't be topped up via chapter select**.

## Achievement Overview

| Category | Count | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Story | 14 | Unlocked automatically for completing each chapter |
| Collectible | 6 | Charms / blades / artefacts / echoes / mastery branches |
| Combat | 12 | Require specific kills or actions |
| Misc | 4 | Specific scene interactions / easter eggs |

## ⚠️ Missables to Watch For

| Achievement | Why it's easy to miss |
|---|---|
| **Like A Warrior** (165 points to unlock 6 mastery branches) | ⚠️ **The only permanently missable achievement**: Resonance Points don't carry over via chapter select — miss 165 in one run and you must restart |
| **Sea You Later** (see the turtle) | Chapter 3 opening beach, opposite the main path, easy to walk straight past |
| **Theater Enthusiast** (watch the full puppet show) | Chapter 2 carnival — leaving early doesn't count, watch for about a minute |

All other achievements can be collected via chapter select.

## Platinum / Full-Achievement Route

1. **First run**: grab every Resonance Point (target 165), collect charms/blades/artefacts as you go, score 1 kill with each new blade
2. **Post-game chapter select**: charms, blades, artefacts and echoes are all collectible; the select screen shows progress
3. **Combat achievements**: use chapter select to return to suitable enemy groups and deliberately set them up (e.g. Overboard at cliffs, They Were People in Chapter 13's petrified enemies)
4. **Misc**: Sea You Later and Theater Enthusiast are the easiest to miss — watch for them on the first run

> There are no online or difficulty-gated achievements — everything is doable on Narrative difficulty with the invincibility option.
