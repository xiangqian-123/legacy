Stuck? This page collects the **quick solution** for every puzzle, organized by chapter — just copy the answer. For the underlying mechanics, see each chapter guide.

> The game's puzzles boil down to two kinds: **light reflection** (shine the orb at mirrors/pedestals) and **symbol matching** (read the wall symbols, rotate pedestals / jump floor tiles). Once you know the pattern, none of them are hard.

## Which chapter are you stuck on?

Chapters with dedicated puzzles are **Ch4, Ch5, Ch7, Ch10 and Ch11** (the rest have no standalone puzzles — just play through):

- [Chapter 4 From Hell It Rose](/en/guide/chapter-4-from-hell-it-rose) — three-symbol / mirror / maze / spikes / bull mirror
- [Chapter 5 Desolation It Wrought](/en/guide/chapter-5-desolation-it-wrought) — door puzzle / scarab floor / statue door / mirror room
- [Chapter 7 Death It Sowed](/en/guide/chapter-7-death-it-sowed) — Black Temple skull door / light puzzle
- [Chapter 10 Parallel Paths](/en/guide/chapter-10-parallel-paths) — door puzzle (three pedestals + 6 symbols)
- [Chapter 11 Patera](/en/guide/chapter-11-patera) — light beam / crank / lens

## By Puzzle Type

- **Light/beam puzzles**: shine the orb at mirrors and pedestals to reflect light → Ch4 mirror, Ch10 door, Ch11 beam
- **Sphere/three-color puzzles**: rotate the orb's three colors to match gems → Ch5 door, Ch7 light
- **Symbol/rune puzzles**: read wall symbols, rotate pedestals / jump floor tiles → Ch4 three-symbol, Ch5 scarab floor
- **Hero Tomb puzzles**: three-color light opens the tomb for a blade → Ch5 Greek Sword, Ch6 Sica, Ch7 Khopesh, Ch10 Xiphos

## All Puzzles (by Chapter)

## Ch4 From Hell It Rose

### Three-Symbol Puzzle (door)
Shine the orb at the pedestal and follow the blue line on the floor to the symbol on the wall, then rotate all 3 pedestals to match.
**Answer (left to right): Y - C - ↑**

### Mirror Puzzle
Stand on the floor plate to make the light appear, then reflect in order:
**Left mirror → right mirror → front-left mirror → front-right mirror → door symbol**

### Maze Puzzle (light symbols)
**Left = "O" / Right = "I"**

### Spike Traps
- Platform 1: left - left - left
- Platform 2: right - middle (jump over) - middle
- Spike wall: interact with the third wall slot from the left

### Bull Mirror Puzzle
The white floor symbols (on red bases) pair up two at a time; the mirrors shine on the matching wall symbols:
- Pair 1 (bell + T): right mirror on the 1st icon on the front-left wall, left mirror on the 3rd on the back-right wall
- Pair 2 (reverse-S + triangle): left mirror on the 3rd on the front-right wall, right mirror on the 4th on the front-left wall
- After the bull lowers its hand, shine its armband for the new symbols "h" and "w": left mirror on the 4th on the front-right wall, middle mirror on the 1st on the front-right wall

## Ch5 Desolation It Wrought

### Door Puzzle (first door)
Interact with the central pedestal in the room, stand on the right floor plate, and rotate the orb's three colors to match the gems on the door.

### Scarab Floor Symbols (dice door)
Follow the 5 floor symbols to jump to safe platforms; a wrong jump kills you:
**Dots(2) - Kraken(3) - Dots(3) - Flower(2) - Kraken(2) - Dots(2) - Kraken(1, jump left)**

### Statue Door Puzzle (three trials)
Stand on the floor plate lit by the roof beam and match the door's gems with the orb's three colors:
- Trial 1: red = left statue helmet / green = middle / blue = right statue hand
- Trial 2: green = top / blue = bottom / red = right statue eye
- Trial 3: first shine the front-right dust to reveal a gear, use the gear to have Leni hold it, jump the platform and align the right wall

### Mirror Room Puzzle (line O door)
- Red = shine the right-most mirror → shine the right pillar
- Blue = left mirror → back-bottom mirror → middle mirror → left pillar
- Green = back-right mirror → back of pillar

## Ch7 Death It Sowed

### Black Temple Skull Door
Shine the orb at the door to reveal green-dot hints for the press order:
**Right-most skull → 2nd from right → left-most → 2nd from left**

### Black Temple Light Puzzle
Stand in the light and shine the wall to find the blue-icon skull, move the light to it; rotate the base mirror to center and stand on the floor plate:
**Green = center mirror / red = left mirror / blue = right mirror**

## Ch10 Parallel Paths

### Door Puzzle (three pedestals + 6 symbols)
1. Light the waterfall-cave mirror (turn the middle wheel clockwise)
2. Reflect it to the door in sequence
3. After Faro shines the light, stand on the floor plate and watch the blue icon on the green metal
4. Walk to the stele in the light, use the orb to reveal 6 symbols, and slide the red/green/blue crystals on the door to match

## Ch11 Patera

### Light Beam Puzzle
**Release the right lever to let Faro take over, then pull the left lever** to melt the dust.

### Crank Puzzle
Push the weight to the lever to make a light shield → pull the lever to swing the light to the 2nd crank → turn the crank all the way.

### The Last Lens
Stand on the elevator and wait for Faro to lift it, crouch past the petrified enemies, burn the dust off the chain with the orb, and turn the crank to the end.
