The Sica hides in a side path after the Daedalus key in Chapter 6, requiring a three-color light puzzle.

## Overview

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Chapter | Ch6 What Lies Beneath |
| How to get | Hero Tomb #3 (light puzzle) |
| Technique | Focus Rush (kills refill the focus gauge) |
| Linked achievement | Plunderer / Adaptable |

## Location & Solution

Sub-chapter 5 "The architect":

1. After taking Daedalus's key and opening the door, **skip the main path** and turn left into the long tunnel
2. In the room with several light points, climb onto the central platform on the left
3. Align the orb's three colors: green = under the bull pillar / blue = under the pillar you're on / red = the back-right pillar (via mirror)
4. The tomb opens; enter and open the chest

## Tips

- **Focus Rush**: every kill refills your focus gauge — ideal for a "focus-skill spam" build.
- Top choice for players who use focus attacks constantly.
