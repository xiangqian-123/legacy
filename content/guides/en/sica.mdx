The Sica hides in the **Blade Tomb** (Hero Tomb #3), an optional side path in Chapter 6 after you take Daedalus's Key. It's gated behind a three-color light puzzle.

## Location & Solution

1. In "The architect", after taking **Daedalus's Key** and leaving the area overlooking the labyrinth, follow the critical path into the tunnel.
2. Watch for a **small opening on the left** — easy to miss, so don't rush past it. Enter the side passage to reach the optional Blade Tomb.
3. Inside the tomb are pedestals, coloured gems and a central hatch marked with an axe sigil.
4. **Climb to the top of the pillar** — the only spot where the Sphere can refract light onto all three gems.
5. Use **Sophia's Sphere** to cast the three coloured rays (red, blue, green). **Aim the green beam at the green gem first** (under the bull pillar), then rotate the red and blue beams so they reflect off the room's mirrors onto their matching gems.
6. When all three gems are lit at once, the axe-marked hatch opens — enter the crypt and open the chest for the Sica.

## Tips

- **Focus Rush**: every kill refills your focus gauge — ideal for a "focus-skill spam" build.
- Top choice for players who use focus attacks constantly.
