The most frequently asked questions about the game, collected. Each answer links to a deeper page.

## Basics

**Q: Is Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy a prequel or a sequel?**
A: A **prequel** — to A Plague Tale: Requiem, set 15 years earlier, telling Sophia's origin story. [See the prequel page](/en/guide/prequel)

**Q: Does it have Chinese support?**
A: Yes. Simplified Chinese has **full UI + full voice + subtitles** — one of only 6 fully-voiced languages. Traditional Chinese has UI + subtitles (no dubbing). [See the Chinese support page](/en/guide/chinese)

**Q: Is it on Xbox Game Pass?**
A: **Day one on Game Pass**, with Xbox Play Anywhere support.

**Q: Which platforms? Is there a PS4 version?**
A: PS5 / Xbox Series X|S / PC (Steam + Epic). **No PS4 version.**

**Q: How much does it cost?**
A: $49.99 (PC) / $59.99 (console); plus the Heritage Pack DLC (about S$4.50).

## Gameplay

**Q: How long to beat?**
A: Main story 12–14 hours; full collectibles ~16–19 hours; platinum ~18–22 hours. [See the game length page](/en/guide/length)

**Q: Is it like the first two games?**
A: Quite different. This one is an **action-adventure** — dynamic melee plus environmental puzzles, compared by media to "Uncharted / Tomb Raider," no longer stealth-survival.

**Q: Are there still rats?**
A: The series' signature rat swarms are replaced here. A classic community joke: "there's only one rat in the whole game" — players coming for the rat-sea should adjust expectations.

**Q: Can you respec skills?**
A: Yes, **free respec anytime** in the Skills menu, no penalty. [See the skills page](/en/guide/skills)

## Collectibles & Achievements

**Q: Why do my Resonance Points disappear?**
A: Points **don't carry over via chapter select** — replaying an earlier chapter resets the total to what it was then, and dying loses the points collected since the last checkpoint. Grab every one you see. [See Collectibles Overview](/en/guide/collectibles)

**Q: Can charms and blades be collected after finishing?**
A: Yes. Both can be collected via **chapter select**, and the select screen shows each chapter's charm progress.

**Q: Does the DLC Hero's Blade count toward trophies?**
A: **It counts toward no trophy.** The Adaptable trophy's "1 kill per blade" includes the starting Rusty Sword, but not the DLC weapon.

**Q: How many achievements are there?**
A: 36 Steam achievements (including the platinum/full completion). See [Collectibles Overview](/en/guide/collectibles) for the collectible ones.

## Buying

**Q: Is it worth buying?**
A: Recommended for series fans and action-adventure lovers; try it risk-free on Game Pass if unsure. [See the buying guide](/en/guide/worth-it)

**Q: Is there a physical collector's edition?**
A: Yes, a Collector Box (Europe pre-order, ships end of October).
