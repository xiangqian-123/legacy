The combat is a dynamic melee system, a complete break from the stealth-heavy first two games. Core loop: **parry for stagger → kick/combo for damage → Focus Attack to clear the crowd**. This page breaks down the mechanics.

## Basic Actions & Reads

| Action | Notes |
|---|---|
| Attack | Basic sword hits, chainable |
| **Parry** | Well-timed parries deflect attacks and cause stagger; **yellow attacks** can be parried, **red attacks** must be dodged until later |
| **Dodge** | Double-tap dodge for a second dodge (requires the Hero's Step skill) |
| **Kick** | The second kick of a combo can push **non-staggered** enemies — instant kills near cliffs/hazards |
| **Throw** | Pick up throwables in the environment, hit an enemy and follow with a combo |

## Focus Attack

The core resource system of combat:

- **Unlocked automatically, no skill points needed**
- Build the **Focus Gauge** through attacks, parries and dodges
- When full, unleash a **wide sweep** that hits multiple nearby enemies
- 💡 Don't save it for bosses — it pays off most when surrounded, and it recharges fast

## Enemy Attack Reads (Key)

| Attack type | Color | Response |
|---|---|---|
| Normal | None/white | Parry or dodge |
| Heavy | Yellow | Can be parried |
| Unblockable | Red | Dodge only at first; parryable after Aegean Deflect mastery |

> Red-attack parry mastery is the game-changer against Brutes — the core value of the parry build.

## Throwables & Environment

Combat scenes are littered with throwables (jars, objects). With the **Fated Cast** skill, you can store a throwable on your belt and fire it with R3 anytime — effectively a built-in ranged option.

## Combat Styles at a Glance

| Style | Core skills | Idea |
|---|---|---|
| Parry | Aegean Deflect + Echo's Return | Perfect parry → doubled stagger → grab and hurl into crowds for group stagger |
| Dodge | Hero's Step + Fated Cast | Double-dodge to kite → throwable attrition → combo finisher |
| Focus | Sica + Focus Rush | Kills refill the focus gauge, spamming focus skills |

## Theseus Flashback Combat (Note)

Some flashback segments put you in control of Theseus, who has his **own combat rhythm and Focus Attack**, but Sophia's skill upgrades **don't carry over**. Players who rely on red-attack parries must re-adapt when the character switches — start by dodging to learn the rhythm.
