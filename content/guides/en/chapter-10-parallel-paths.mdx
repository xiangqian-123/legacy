Chapter 10 Parallel Paths enters Daedalus' workshop. There are **33 collectibles**, including a complex mirror-light puzzle.

## Collectible Overview

| Type | Count | Details |
|---|---|---|
| Blade | 1 | Xiphos |
| Artefact | 4 | Sun Dial / Terra Incognita / Thakourth Ball / Impossible Mechanism |
| Charm | 3 | Beads #5 / Trinket #4 / Pierced Coin #5 |
| Resonance Points | 25 | — |

## Route (5 sub-chapters)

1. **The bridge** — riverside collection (dense)
2. **In the plunderers' wake** — Xiphos + mirror-light puzzle
3. **Daedalus' workshop** — workshop puzzle
4. **The test Trial** — no collectibles
5. **A light that burns** — get the orb upgrade

## Puzzle Solution

- **Door puzzle (3 pedestals)**: first light the waterfall-cave mirror (turn the center wheel clockwise) and reflect it to the door. After Faro shines the light, stand on the floor plate to see the blue icon on the green metal; Sophia memorizes it. Walk to the stele in the light and use the orb to reveal 6 symbols, then slide the red/green/blue crystals on the door to match.

## Blade Location

- **Xiphos**: the bull-statue area — first smash the jar to reveal a blue gem, use the pedestal to turn on the light, stand on the right-corner light platform and align the three colors (red = left / green = middle / blue = right · hidden under the jar) to open the tomb. Technique "Parry Break": parries stagger much harder, but you **cannot block**.

## Artefact Locations

- **Sun Dial** (Past): at the start, squeeze through, follow the cliff to the ladder, then squeeze right.
- **Terra Incognita** (Present): after the log slide, between two broken jars on the right edge.
- **Thakourth Ball** (Present): after dismantling the scaffolding, at the end of the right riverside edge.
- **Impossible Mechanism** (Past): in the workshop, down the stairs, right across the bridge, in the corner.

## Charm Locations

- **Beads #5**: at the 3 white dots, walk straight 180° to the riverside crate.
- **Trinket #4**: to the right of the camp's second tent.
- **Pierced Coin #5**: the workshop's right stairs — pull the mirror hanging on the chain to drop the weight and open the tomb.

## Chapter Achievement

- Parallel Paths (complete Chapter 10)
