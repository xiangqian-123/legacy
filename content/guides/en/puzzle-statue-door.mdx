The statue door puzzle in the four-symbol trials of Chapter 5 — three trials total.

## Quick Solution

Each time, stand on the floor plate lit by the roof beam and align the orb's three colors to the door's gems:

1. **Trial 1**: red = left statue helmet / green = middle / blue = right statue hand
2. **Trial 2**: green = top / blue = bottom / red = right statue eye
3. **Trial 3**: first shine the front-right dust to reveal a cogwheel, use it to have Leni hold it, jump the platform, then align the right wall

## How It Works

- The three trials follow the same routine: stand on the plate → switch the three colors → rotate to match the gems
- Trial 3 adds a "burn the dust to reveal the cogwheel" step first — don't skip it
- After each alignment, **run-jump over the spikes** (hold run before jumping off)

> Why you're stuck: on Trial 3, aligning without first burning the dust for the cogwheel. Burn the front-right dust, let Leni hold the cog, then proceed.
