Chapter 6 What Lies Beneath is the creature-chase chapter. There are **28 collectibles**. Plenty of Resonance Points hide along the escape route, so it's easy to miss them while running.

## Collectible Overview

| Type | Count | Details |
|---|---|---|
| Blade | 1 | Sica |
| Artefact | 2 | Minoan Queen / Corrupted Mosaic |
| Charm | 3 | Seal #3 / Bead #3 / Pierced Coin #2 |
| Resonance Points | 22 | — |

## Route (5 sub-chapters)

1. **Lost** — cave escape
2. **The creature** — first chase
3. **False hope** — chase + water wheel
4. **Minoan machinery** — mirrors open the path
5. **The architect** — Daedalus shortcut + Sica

## Blade Location

- **Sica**: after taking the Daedalus key and opening the door, skip the main path and enter the left side-tunnel to a room with several light points. Stand on the central platform's left pillar and align the orb's three colors (green = under the bull pillar / blue = under the pillar you're on / red = the back-right pillar via mirror) to open the tomb. Technique "Focus Rush": kills refill your focus gauge.

## Artefact Locations

- **Minoan Queen** (Past): the water-wheel area, jump the gap under the broken wheel, in the cave on the right cliff.
- **Corrupted Mosaic** (Past): the Daedalus shortcut — facing the stairs you came from, at the left dark-path doorway.

## Charm Locations

- **Seal #3**: the mirror room, smash the crate on the right at the corridor's end.
- **Bead #3**: the architect's room, in the crate in the dark path to the right of the stairs.
- **Pierced Coin #2**: jump off the cliff beside the left charm, go straight to the door at the end, interact with the axe chalice.

## Chase-Segment Resonance Point Reminder

During the creature chase, one point hides below the cliff at the second lamp and another inside the broken pipe on the right. Grabbing them while fleeing is tricky; miss them and you'll have to redo the segment (Resonance Points can't be topped up later).

## Chapter Achievement

- What Lies Beneath (complete Chapter 6)
