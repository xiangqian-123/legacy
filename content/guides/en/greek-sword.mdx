The Greek Sword is the first blade that requires a **full light puzzle** to obtain, located in the Chapter 5 four-symbol trial area.

## Overview

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Chapter | Ch5 Desolation It Wrought |
| How to get | Hero Tomb #2 (light puzzle) |
| Technique | Spinning Slash (crits become a 360° whirlwind) |
| Linked achievement | Plunderer / Adaptable |

## Location & Solution

On the **dice-door** path of the four-symbol trials, after reaching the camp with the skeleton pillar:

1. Find the camp's skeleton pillar and step onto the stone floor plate beside it, on the left
2. Use the orb's three light colors to align with the pillar's colored gems (the bull pillar)
3. Once aligned, the axe symbol on the ground opens, revealing stairs into the tomb
4. Descend and open the large chest

## Tips

- **Spinning Slash is a game-changer**: crits no longer use the dagger — they become a 360° whirlwind that hits all surrounding enemies.
- Best for **3+ enemy fights**; switch to it when you're surrounded.
