Chapter 3 Those Before Us has **9 points**, all **scattered pickups** — 2 of them are easy to miss.

| Points this chapter | Running total | Miss risk |
|---|---|---|
| 9 | 14 | Medium (2 easy to miss) |

## Point Locations

| # | Sub-chapter | Location |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 Paradise | Spawn beach — walk the shoreline the other way, inside the wrecked boat (across from the big green gate) |
| 2 | 1 Paradise | Climb the back-left cliffs with the grappling hook, slide down, on the ground (by where you push the log for Leni) |
| 3 | 1 Paradise | Jump the gap to the ruins, go straight to the sea cliffs, on a tree stump |
| 4 | 1 Paradise | Tallest ruin in the corner — grapple up to the viewing platform (story path) |
| 5 | 1 Paradise | Through the abandoned camp, on the right just before the ladder |
| 6 | 2 Vestiges | Big open area — drop below the wooden platform |
| 7 | 2 Vestiges | Lower ruins right corner (by the Beads #2 charm; can drop through the broken ceiling) |
| 8 | 2 Vestiges | Big statue area — up the left hill, in a basket at the top of the ruins |
| 9 | 5 Start of trials | After rafting across the water, in the temple by a broken statue head |

## ⚠️ Miss Risk

- **Point 1 (wrecked boat)**: opposite the main path at spawn — easy to walk straight past.
- **Point 2**: hidden below the slope after climbing the cliff.

> No automatic combat rewards this chapter — all 9 must be found. The main path hands you about 7; points 1 and 2 need a deliberate detour.
