The Falchion is Sophia's first "proper" weapon, obtained automatically in the Chapter 2 Felons carnival.

## Overview

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Chapter | Ch2 Felons |
| How to get | Automatic (story) |
| Technique | Momentum Strike (bonus damage after a crit) |
| Linked achievement | Adaptable (at least 1 kill per blade) |

## Location

In sub-chapter 2 "The carnival", it's obtained automatically when you interact with the barrel by the boat at the carnival's water edge — this **also unlocks the grapnel** (the wall-climbing tool).

## Hero's Blade (DLC)

Pre-order Heritage Pack owners get the **Hero's Blade** at the same spot, with a technique identical to the Falchion (effectively a reskin). Note: **the DLC Hero's Blade doesn't count toward any trophy**.

## Tips

- Bonus damage after a crit makes it a solid general weapon for a crit build.
- Score one kill immediately after getting each new blade to avoid redoing the Adaptable achievement later.
