The first puzzle of Chapter 4 — copy the answer if you're stuck.

## Quick Solution

1. Shine the orb (R1/RB) at the pedestals — **blue lines** appear on the floor, connecting to wall symbols
2. Follow each blue line to see its symbol
3. Rotate the 3 pedestals to match their symbols
4. **Answer (left to right): Y - C - ↑**

## How It Works

This is a symbol-matching puzzle: each pedestal connects via a blue floor line to a wall symbol, and you simply rotate the pedestal to show that same symbol. Left to right they correspond to "Y", "C" and "↑".

> Why you're stuck: rotating pedestals without first shining the orb to see the lines. Shine first, read the lines, then rotate.
