There's no class system here — a "build" is just skill order × weapon × charms. Three main routes below; pick one and follow it.

## Route 1: Balanced (recommended for beginners)

**Skill order**: Aegean Deflect (max red-attack mastery) → Hero's Step → Echo's Return → Bullbreaker Kick / Fated Cast

- **Weapon**: Falchion (bonus damage after crit) or Kopis (combo stagger)
- **Charms**: Beads (archers miss more) + Arm Bands (wider parry window)
- **For**: first-time players who want steady progress

## Route 2: Parry build (aggressive)

**Skill order**: Aegean Deflect → Echo's Return → Hero's Step → Bullbreaker Kick

- **Weapon**: **Xiphos** (Parry Break, much stronger parry stagger at the cost of no block) + Khopesh for bosses
- **Charms**: Arm Bands (wider parry window — core)
- **For**: players who want high risk / high reward and are comfortable parrying

## Route 3: Dodge build (safe)

**Skill order**: Hero's Step → Echo's Return → Aegean Deflect → Fated Cast

- **Weapon**: Sica (Focus Rush refills focus on kill)
- **Charms**: Pierced Coins (chance to keep focus charge) + Trinkets (chance to survive fatal blows)
- **For**: players who like kiting and focus-skill spam

## Quick Reference

| Route | Core skills | Main weapon | Idea |
|---|---|---|---|
| Balanced | Aegean Deflect + Hero's Step | Falchion / Kopis | Parry → stagger → kick |
| Parry | Aegean Deflect + Echo's Return | Xiphos | Perfect parry → doubled stagger → hurl into crowds |
| Dodge | Hero's Step + Echo's Return | Sica | Double-dodge to kite → focus-skill spam |

## Key Reminders

- **Free respec anytime** — try all three and settle on what feels right
- ⚠️ In Theseus flashback fights, **Sophia's upgrades don't carry over** — red-attack parry is unavailable, so dodge first to learn the rhythm
- The parry build's Xiphos **removes your block** — parry-only, avoid if you struggle with timing
