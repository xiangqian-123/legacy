Chapter 7 Death It Sowed crosses a massive battlefield into the Black Temple. There are **3 puzzles**, **32 collectibles** and one chapter achievement. Chapter 8 Resonance has no collectibles, so it's folded into this chapter's progress.

## Route (5 sub-chapters)

1. **Back to Leni** — follow the dirt path to a huge pit of ruins and water. Grapnel along the ledge, descend into the pit with a second hook (grab the Resonance Point just before it) and enter the bone chamber. Solve the two-crank gate puzzle (below), then defeat the soldier beyond.
2. **The whole damn army** — the beach battlefield, the collectible-dense stretch. Keep right into the ruins, clear the soldiers, grapnel the door and crawl under the gap for a Resonance Point. Drop into the hole and follow the underground passage out; skip the dead-end stairs and keep left past the ruined minotaur's head. Two soldiers guard the temple doors. Along the way: the Tarot Card, Bead #4, Kill Onyx, the Khopesh (white-pillar area) and Arm Band #4.
3. **A stage of skulls** — the Black Temple. Solve the skull door, then the light puzzle. Before entering, go right by the cliff edge down a narrow path for the Stained Mosaic. Inside: the room of suspended jars, then the circular room.
4. **The arena** — a circular room with no collectibles. Kick enemies into the central pit (grapple/kick); save the armoured mace brute for last.
5. **Its ruins** — the waterway. After the big drop, slide under the broken gate, swing two grapnels and jump right for Pierced Coin #3; before the zipline, turn right onto the wooden platform for the Kouros Puppet; after the zipline, climb and turn left for Trinket #3. Up the debris ramp, jump the gap platforms to the twisted tree.

## Puzzle Solutions

### Gate Puzzle (Back to Leni, bone chamber)

The gate has no light on it at first — two cranks fix that:

1. Use the **crank at the back of the room** (behind the rocks) to move its lens so the beam passes through the lens directly in front of the door.
2. Use the **crank in the middle of the room** to line the second lens up with the lens above the door — the light lands on the podium.
3. **Stand on the podium** and use the Sphere's Splitting Light.
4. Align the three coloured beams with the gemstones on the door. The gate opens.

### Black Temple Skull Door

Shine the orb at the door to reveal green-dot hints, then press the skulls in the revealed order:

**Right-most skull → 2nd from right → left-most → 2nd from left**

A wrong press resets the sequence — just press again.

### Black Temple Light Puzzle

1. Defeat the first enemy group in the circular room. **Stand in the light on the right** and shine the orb at the wall to reveal a blue-icon skull — interact with it to move the light source.
2. Kill the soldiers who burst in (including the archer).
3. Repeat on the **left** pedestal to reveal a second skull.
4. Interact with the pedestal by the doors **twice** — the second interaction moves the mirror near the hole (left of the green gem) to the centre.
5. Stand on the floor plate and switch to coloured beams: **green = centre mirror (hits the gem below) / red = left mirror / blue = right mirror**.
6. Platforms rise — jump and drop into the central hole, grapple down, slide under the broken metal gate, and catch yourself with a midair grapple.

## Stuck? The Usual Culprits

- **Skull door won't open**: pressing skulls by feel without shining the door first. Shine → read the green dots → press in order.
- **Gate puzzle**: the back crank is easy to miss — it's behind the rocks at the back of the room, not near the door.
- **Light puzzle**: interacting with the pedestal only once. The mirror only moves to the centre on the **second** interaction, and you must stand on the floor plate before switching beams.

## Blade Location

- **Khopesh**: in the battlefield's white-pillar area, by the skull in the back-left corner — stand on the metal plate and align the three light colors to open the door. Technique "Deep Bleed": the final hit of a combo deals damage over time, a boss-fight workhorse.

## Artefact Locations

- **Tarot Card** (Present): top-right corner of the beach, interact with the corpse.
- **Kill Onyx** (Past): after the underground section, turn right around the corner, crouch past the rock, in front of the grate behind the ruins.
- **Stained Mosaic** (Past): turn right at the hilltop temple entrance.
- **Kouros Puppet** (Past): in the waterway, when you should turn left to the zipline, first turn right onto the wooden platform.

## Charm Locations

- **Bead #4**: the beach, in the crate by the second boat.
- **Arm Band #4**: after the double-grapnel gap, take the right path down to the beach, clear the enemies, check the corner crate.
- **Pierced Coin #3**: after dropping down the big hole, slide past the door, swing 2 grapnels, then jump right.
- **Bracelet #4**: immediately up the next staircase, on the left.
- **Trinket #3**: after the zipline, climb the platform and turn left to the crate.

## FAQ

**Q: What is the Chapter 7 skull door press order?**
A: Shine the orb at the Black Temple door to reveal green-dot hints, then press: right-most skull → 2nd from right → left-most → 2nd from left. A wrong press resets the sequence.

**Q: How do you solve the Death It Sowed light puzzle?**
A: Stand in the light on the right and shine the wall to reveal a blue-icon skull; repeat on the left pedestal for a second skull; interact with the pedestal by the doors twice so the mirror moves to the centre; then stand on the floor plate and align green = centre mirror, red = left mirror, blue = right mirror. Platforms rise and you drop into the central hole.

**Q: How many collectibles are in Chapter 7 Death It Sowed?**
A: 32 — 1 blade (Khopesh), 4 artefacts, 5 charms and 22 Resonance Points. None are missable; Chapter 8 has no collectibles of its own.
