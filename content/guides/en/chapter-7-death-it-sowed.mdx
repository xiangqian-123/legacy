Chapter 7 Death It Sowed crosses a massive battlefield into the Black Temple. There are **32 collectibles**. Chapter 8 Resonance has none, so it's folded into this chapter's progress.

## Route (5 sub-chapters)

1. **Back to Leni** — lever puzzle
2. **The whole damn army** — beach battlefield (collectible-dense)
3. **A stage of skulls** — Black Temple skull-door puzzle
4. **The arena** — no collectibles
5. **Its ruins** — waterway / ruins

## Puzzle Solutions

- **Black Temple skull door**: shine the orb at the door to reveal green-dot hints for the press order: right-most skull → second from the right → left-most → second from the left.
- **Black Temple light puzzle**: stand in the light and shine the wall to find the blue-icon skull, then move the light to it; rotate the base mirror to center, stand on the floor plate and align the three colors (green = center mirror / red = left mirror / blue = right mirror).

## Blade Location

- **Khopesh**: in the battlefield's white-pillar area, by the skull in the back-left corner — stand on the metal plate and align the three light colors to open the door. Technique "Deep Bleed": the final hit of a combo deals damage over time, a boss-fight workhorse.

## Artefact Locations

- **Tarot Card** (Present): top-right corner of the beach, interact with the corpse.
- **Kill Onyx** (Past): after the underground section, turn right around the corner, crouch past the rock, in front of the grate behind the ruins.
- **Stained Mosaic** (Past): turn right at the hilltop temple entrance.
- **Kouros Puppet** (Past): in the waterway, when you should turn left to the zipline, first turn right onto the wooden platform.

## Charm Locations

- **Bead #4**: the beach, in the crate by the second boat.
- **Arm Band #4**: after the double-grapnel gap, take the right path down to the beach, clear the enemies, check the corner crate.
- **Pierced Coin #3**: after dropping down the big hole, slide past the door, swing 2 grapnels, then jump right.
- **Bracelet #4**: immediately up the next staircase, on the left.
- **Trinket #3**: after the zipline, climb the platform and turn left to the crate.
