The Khopesh is in the Chapter 7 battlefield, hidden in a Hero Tomb beside a giant skull — a **boss-fight workhorse**.

## Overview

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Chapter | Ch7 Death It Sowed |
| How to get | Hero Tomb #4 (light puzzle) |
| Technique | Deep Bleed (final combo hit applies damage over time) |
| Linked achievement | Plunderer / Adaptable |

## Location & Solution

Sub-chapter 2 "The whole damn army" battlefield:

1. In the large open area with white pillars, find the giant skull in the back-left corner
2. Step onto the metal plate beside the skull (slightly off to the side, not straight on)
3. Use the orb to align the three light colors to the matching gems
4. Open the tomb and claim the blade

## Tips

- **Deep Bleed**: the final combo hit causes a serious wound that bleeds over time.
- **Best for boss fights** — sustained damage plus safe positioning, especially effective against the Minotaur.
