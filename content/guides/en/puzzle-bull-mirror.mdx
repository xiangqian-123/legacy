The bull mirror puzzle in Chapter 4's Theseus sub-chapter — the most complex one this chapter.

## Quick Solution

White floor symbols (red base, blue outline) pair up two at a time; shine both mirrors at the matching wall symbols simultaneously:

1. **Pair 1 (bell + T)**: right mirror on the 1st icon of the front-left wall, left mirror on the 3rd icon of the back-right wall
2. **Pair 2 (reverse-S + triangle)**: left mirror on the 3rd icon of the front-right wall, right mirror on the 4th icon of the front-left wall
3. After the bull lowers its hands, shine its armband for "h" and "w": left mirror on the 4th of the front-right wall, middle mirror on the 1st of the front-right wall
4. Use the pedestal that appears

## How It Works

- The ground symbols with blue outlines are the current pair to match
- Each wall has copies of the ground symbols (some missing, but in the same order)
- Shine both mirrors at the paired symbols simultaneously — solve the two pairs separately

> Why you're stuck: missing the "pair up" rule and thinking one symbol = one mirror. The key is both mirrors must shine on the paired symbols at the same time.
